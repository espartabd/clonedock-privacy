CloneDock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

CloneDock ("the app", "we") is a multi-account workspace that lets you run several web-based messaging and social accounts side by side in isolated sessions. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information we collect

CloneDock does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Data stored on your device

When you sign in to third-party websites (for example messaging or social media services) inside CloneDock, your login sessions, cookies, and app settings are stored locally on your computer only, in separate isolated containers for each account. This data never leaves your device through CloneDock and is not sent to us.

3. Third-party services

The websites you open inside CloneDock (such as Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram and others) are operated by third parties. Any information you enter on those sites is governed by their own privacy policies. CloneDock is not affiliated with or endorsed by these services.

4. Email features

If you use an optional feature that sends email, the message is sent directly from your device using the details you provide. We do not store or read these messages.

5. Deleting your data

You can remove any account from within the app, or uninstall CloneDock, which deletes all locally stored sessions and settings.

6. Children

CloneDock is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
